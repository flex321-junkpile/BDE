Common Police Interaction Best Practices

A focused 100-question training on how to stay safe and calm during any law enforcement encounter — hands, words, body language.

← Back to Training Hub

About This Training

What This Is

The goal of every police interaction should be simple: everyone gets home safely. This training builds real-world tools for hand position, body language, communication, and de-escalation. Winning the moment on the roadside almost never works — winning the long game does. Court is for arguments; the encounter is for compliance and de-escalation.

What's Covered

  1. Foundational Safety PrinciplesComply now, contest later. Get home first.
  2. Hands, Body, and MovementVisible hands, slow motion, narrated actions.
  3. Communication: Tone, Words, SilenceOpening lines, respectful tone, invoking rights out loud.
  4. Vehicle StopsRight-shoulder pull-over, documents, passengers, and step-out orders.
  5. Pedestrian & Public EncountersConsensual vs. detained, frisks, and group situations.
  6. Recording & DocumentationLegal recording, cloud upload, badge numbers, and injury documentation.
  7. Passengers, Children, Multiple OfficersFamily calm, addressing the lead officer, and passenger rules.
  8. Firearms & Legal CarryImmediate disclosure, permit presentation, and precise compliance.
  9. When Things Go Wrong / Post-EncounterComplaints, internal affairs, civilian review, and civil rights attorneys.
