BDE Training Hub

Ten focused, standalone trainings. Take one, take them all — earn a certificate for each.

How This Works

Each training is a standalone 100-question course you can complete on your own time. Every training earns its own certificate of completion when you score 80% or higher. Your progress saves automatically — pause and come back later on the same device.

10 trainings
100 questions each
5 hr per training
80% to earn each certificate
1 cert per training
