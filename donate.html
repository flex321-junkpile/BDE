Why Your Gift Matters

BDE is more than an organization — it's a movement. Every dollar you give goes directly toward developing leaders, serving communities, and forging the kind of brotherhood that changes lives.

We are a nonprofit organization run by brothers who show up — in classrooms, shelters, and neighborhoods. Your support makes that possible.

  • Funding community service initiatives and outreach events
  • Supporting leadership development workshops and mentorship programs
  • Covering transportation and logistics for youth-facing activities
  • Keeping membership accessible for brothers at every income level
  • Growing our reach so more men can find their brotherhood
