Community Service

Service that strengthens the community — and the brothers who do it.

Our Services

Four ways BDE shows up for the community. Earn service hours, build connection, support wellness.

Online Webinar

A required orientation webinar covering BDE's mission, the standards we operate by, and how community service hours are tracked and verified. Complete this first before participating in any in-person service.

Community Service Outreach

Hands-on outreach events in the local community — neighborhood cleanups, supply drives, youth mentorship pop-ups, and partnership events with community organizations.

Mental Health Hike

A guided group hike built around the idea that movement and nature open men up. Brothers walk, talk, and process together in a low-pressure outdoor setting.

Mental Health Group Session

A facilitated group conversation — the No Filter Zone format — focused on mental wellness, accountability, and peer support among men who agree to show up honestly.
