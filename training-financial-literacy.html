Financial Literacy & Money Management

A focused 100-question training on budgeting, banking, credit, debt, court fines, saving, and building real financial stability. Earn your BDE certificate of completion.

About This Training

What This Is

Money problems are one of the biggest reasons people stay stuck or end up back in trouble. This training gives you practical, plain-English tools to take control of your money — budgeting, banking, building credit, handling debt and court fines, and saving for the future. Financial stability is freedom, and it's a skill you can learn.

What's Covered

  1. Budgeting Basics Income vs. expenses, needs vs. wants, tracking spending, and the 50/30/20 plan.
  2. Banking & Accounts Checking vs. savings, avoiding fees and overdrafts, direct deposit, and second-chance accounts.
  3. Credit & Credit Scores What a score is, how to build and rebuild it, secured cards, and reading your report.
  4. Debt & Predatory Lending Good vs. bad debt, payday and title loans, APR, payoff strategies, and your rights with collectors.
  5. Court Fines, Fees & Restitution Payment plans, restitution, protecting your license, and budgeting court debt as a priority.
  6. Saving & Emergency Funds Why and how to save, emergency funds, automating savings, and saving on a low income.
  7. Earning, Taxes & Building Wealth W-2 vs. 1099, taxes and refunds, the EITC, retirement accounts, and long-term wealth habits.
